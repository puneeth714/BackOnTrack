✦ Target Domain Scope
6 Core Enterprise Domains.
Unlimited Possibilities.
We define the playing field—you decide what to build. Select any domain below to view its focus area and build any product or platform of your choice.

Domain 01
BFSI AI Problems
Banking & Financial Services
Transform banking, financial services, and insurance ecosystems with artificial intelligence. From risk infrastructure to customer experiences, build any AI solution that targets inefficiencies across the financial landscape.

Builder Scope: Identify any key challenge across banking, wealth management, insurance, or compliance and engineer an AI-first product or platform to address it.
A Note for Builders: While Open Innovation gives you full wildcard freedom, building for specific industry domains (BFSI, EdTech, Sales, HR, Innovation) is equally vital to solving core enterprise demand. Think out of the box—both targeted industry solutions and open wildcard ideas are key to the festival!
